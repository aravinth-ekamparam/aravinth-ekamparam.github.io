Academic Profile

Environmental Geochemistry & Water Purification

Dr. Aravinth Siva Subramaniam Ekamparam is a Research Scientist at Detoxyfi Technologies. He earned his PhD from the Department of Civil Engineering and Environmental Geochemistry Laboratory at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur), followed by postdoctoral research at Oregon State University (OSU).

His core expertise encompasses mineral phase transformations (such as calcite to fluorapatite), kinetic modeling of fluoride and uranium mobilization, and engineered pretreatment strategies for drywell managed aquifer recharge (MAR).

Primary Research Vectors

Environmental Geochemistry

Mineral-fluid interfacial thermodynamics, calcite dissolution, and phase transformation to fluorapatite.

Groundwater Quality

Assessment of geogenic contaminants (Fluoride, Uranium, Manganese) mobilization in aquifer systems.

Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR)

Developing engineered pretreatment approaches to prevent physical and biological clogging in recharge drywells.

Water Treatment & Sorbents

Evaluation of sorbents (calcium phosphate, activated alumina sludge) for fluoride attenuation.

Pretreatment Technologies

Multi-stage filtration beds engineered to minimize foulants and extend membrane lifetime.

Contaminant Fate & Transport

Flow-through reactor kinetics, geochemical speciation, and porous media transport simulations.
